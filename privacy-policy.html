Privacy Policy - SAV Manager Pro

Application: SAV Manager Pro

Developer: TATAZ TECH

Last updated: October 2026

1. Data We Collect

SAV Manager Pro collects information necessary to provide its maintenance and after-sales service management features.

Depending on the features used, this information may include:

  • User name and email address;
  • Account and company information;
  • Customer information entered by users;
  • Equipment information;
  • Maintenance and service intervention data;
  • Inventory, spare parts quantities, and inventory movement data;
  • Photos voluntarily added by users;
  • Files and documents voluntarily added by users;
  • Other information entered by users for maintenance activities.

Photos, files, and documents are optional and are collected only when the user chooses to add them.

2. How We Use Data

We use this information to:

  • Create and manage user accounts;
  • Authenticate users;
  • Synchronize company data between authorized users;
  • Manage customers and equipment;
  • Create and manage maintenance interventions;
  • Manage spare parts and inventory;
  • Generate intervention reports;
  • Allow authorized users within the same company to access relevant company information;
  • Maintain the security and proper operation of the application.

3. Firebase and Cloud Services

SAV Manager Pro uses Google Firebase services for user authentication and cloud data synchronization.

Application data may therefore be transmitted between the user's device and Firebase services. Data transmitted between the application and cloud services is protected using encrypted connections.

4. Data Sharing

TATAZ TECH does not sell users' personal data.

Data may be processed by service providers necessary to operate SAV Manager Pro, including Google Firebase for authentication, cloud infrastructure, and synchronization.

Company information may be accessible to authorized users associated with the same company account according to their roles and permissions.

5. Photos, Files and Documents

Users may voluntarily add photos, files, or documents to maintenance records and interventions. These items are used to provide the application features requested by the user.

6. Data Security

We use reasonable technical measures to protect user data against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or loss.

Access to company data is controlled through authenticated user accounts and company-specific access controls.

7. Account and Data Deletion

Users can request deletion of their account directly from SAV Manager Pro:

Settings → Delete my account

Users who no longer have access to the application can find account deletion instructions at:

SAV Manager Pro Account Deletion

When an account is deleted, associated application data is deleted where applicable.

Certain information may be retained when required for legal, accounting, fraud prevention, or security purposes, and only for the required retention period.

8. Advertising

SAV Manager Pro does not display third-party advertising.

9. Children's Privacy

SAV Manager Pro is intended for professional maintenance and after-sales service management and is not designed for children.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when application features, data practices, or legal requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page.
