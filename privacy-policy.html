# Privacy Policy – SAV Manager Pro **Application:** SAV Manager Pro **Developer:** TATAZ TECH **Last updated:** October 2026 ## 1. Data We Collect SAV Manager Pro may collect and store information necessary to provide its maintenance and after-sales service management features. Depending on the features used, this information may include: - User name and email address; - Account and company information; - Customer information entered by users; - Equipment information; - Maintenance and service intervention data; - Inventory, spare parts quantities, and inventory movement data; - Photos added voluntarily to interventions or equipment; - Files and documents voluntarily added by users; - Other information entered by users as part of their maintenance activities. Photos, files, and documents are optional and are collected only when the user chooses to add them. ## 2. How We Use Data The collected data is used to: - Create and manage user accounts; - Authenticate users; - Synchronize company data between authorized users; - Manage customers and equipment; - Create and manage maintenance interventions; - Manage spare parts and inventory; - Generate intervention reports; - Allow authorized members of the same company to access relevant company information; - Maintain the security and proper operation of the application. ## 3. Cloud Storage and Firebase SAV Manager Pro uses Google Firebase services for features such as user authentication and cloud data synchronization. Application data may therefore be transmitted securely between the user's device and Firebase services. Data transmitted between the application and cloud services is protected using encrypted connections. ## 4. Data Sharing TATAZ TECH does not sell users' personal data. Data may be processed by service providers required to operate SAV Manager Pro, such as Google Firebase, for authentication, cloud storage, synchronization, and related application infrastructure. Company information is accessible only to authorized users associated with the same company account, according to their assigned permissions and roles. ## 5. Photos, Files and Documents Users may optionally add photos, files, or documents to support maintenance activities and intervention records. These items are used only to provide the requested application functionality and may be synchronized with the company's cloud data when cloud synchronization is enabled. ## 6. Data Security Reasonable technical measures are used to protect user data against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or loss. Access to company data is controlled through authenticated user accounts and company identifiers. ## 7. Account and Data Deletion Users can request deletion of their account directly from SAV Manager Pro: **Settings → Delete my account** Users who no longer have access to the application can find account deletion instructions at: https://tattaztech.github.io/sav-manager-pro/ When an account or company account is deleted, associated data is deleted where applicable, including user profiles, customers, equipment, interventions, and inventory information. Certain information may be retained when required for legal, accounting, fraud prevention, or security purposes and only for the required retention period. ## 8. Advertising SAV Manager Pro does not display third-party advertising. ## 9. Children's Privacy SAV Manager Pro is intended for professional maintenance and after-sales service management and is not designed for children. ## 10. Changes to This Privacy Policy This Privacy Policy may be updated when application features, data practices, or legal requirements change. The latest version will be made available on this page. ## 11.
